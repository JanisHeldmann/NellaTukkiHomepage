Nella Turkki is a Finnish dancer, choreographer, and theatre director whose work moves at the meeting point of ecology, social justice, and embodied practice. She creates performances, community art projects, and pedagogical frameworks that ask what it means to be a body in relation — to other people, to environments, to the more-than-human world.

Her practice is grounded in applied theatre and somatic approaches, shaped by extensive experience across Finland and internationally. She works with professional companies and with communities who have rarely had access to art-making — holding both with equal seriousness.

Alongside her artistic work, Nella is an active researcher. She has published peer-reviewed work on eco-social justice in dance education and is currently developing a research focus she plans to pursue at doctoral level — exploring how embodied and arts-based practices can transform learning environments and social dynamics.

Academic Practice

Research
& Scholarship

Nella's research practice grows directly from her artistic and pedagogical work. She investigates how embodied and arts-based methods can address eco-social challenges in educational and community contexts — and how movement itself can be a form of knowing.

Her published work focuses on eco-social justice in dance education, examining how somatic awareness shapes learning environments, student agency, and social dynamics. She is developing this into a doctoral research focus.

Future direction

Nella is actively working toward a PhD in education or arts-based practice — pursuing the question of how embodied, ecological approaches can transform how we learn, relate, and act in the world.

Eco-social justice in dance education

How can dance pedagogy address social and ecological questions simultaneously? Research into teaching practices that hold both human and environmental wellbeing.

Embodied learning environments

How somatic awareness, presence, and physical attunement shape the dynamics of learning spaces — in schools, higher education, and community settings.

Arts-based methods & multispecies thinking

Drawing on work like I, Holobiont and Metsänalaiset — using art as a research method for exploring human-environment relations.

Practice & Facilitation

Coaching &
Embodiment

Nella brings her artistic and research practice into workshops, coaching, and service design — offering individuals, communities, and organisations a movement-based approach to presence, group dynamics, and wellbeing. Drawing on somatic inquiry, dance, and applied theatre, she creates conditions for genuine encounter.

Workshops

Somatic and movement-based workshops tailored to context and group — from one-day intensives to longer formats, for communities, educational institutions, and organisations.

Coaching

Individual sessions drawing on embodied awareness and somatic inquiry — for artists, educators, and anyone seeking a body-based approach to their creative or personal challenges.

Service Design

Facilitation and service design that brings embodiment principles into workplace culture, group dynamics, and organisational wellbeing — grounded in Nella's YAMK studies in service design.
