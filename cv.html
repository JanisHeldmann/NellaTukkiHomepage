Research Focus

Eco-social justice in dance education

How embodied and arts-based practices address ecological and social questions simultaneously in educational contexts.

Embodied learning environments

How somatic awareness, presence, and physical attunement shape the dynamics of learning spaces.

Arts-based methods & multispecies thinking

Using art as a research method for exploring human-environment relations — developed through works such as I, Holobiont and Metsänalaiset.

In progress

PhD — Education or Arts-Based Practice

Nella is actively developing a doctoral research proposal on how embodied, ecological approaches can transform learning, relation, and action in the world.
